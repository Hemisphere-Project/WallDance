## 2. Verdict

WallDance is carefully engineered part by part: crash boundaries, atomic config writes, a clean command queue,
deterministic replays, constants annotated with the take and the date that justified them. Three things have
fallen behind the show it is meant for.

1. **The choreography sits where the shipped logic fails by construction** (§3).
2. **The estimator is half right.** The identity-slot layer has the right shape; the tracker beneath it does
   not; the case for replacing it is not proven yet (§4).
3. **No automated gate covers what ships** (§5).

## 3. The production regimes

The owner confirmed on 2026-11-18 that the production has more than two dancers on the wall, sustained contact
and crossings, entries from the roof or the floor, a dancer motionless for 10 s or more, and daylight fading
from dusk to night. October's validation covered solo takes and one April two-dancer proxy.

| Regime | What the shipped code does | Severity | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact and crossings | In a 5 s embrace where YOLO returns one box, the partner's point is gone for 3.8–4.9 s; for 10 s, 8.8–9.9 s. Two boxes at least 0.29 body-heights apart keep both points. A 1 s crossing costs 1.4 s without a plate, nothing with a valid plate. | show-stopper | [regime](audit-2026-11/regime-contact-and-crossings.md) |
| Long stillness | Fine while YOLO is steady. If YOLO drops a dim, motionless dancer for 6 frames, her returning detections are rejected for lack of motion until she moves. A valid empty-wall plate closes the hole. | high | [regime](audit-2026-11/regime-long-stillness.md) |
| More than two dancers | The default cap is 2: a third dancer is tracked and drawn but never sent, with no alert. With more bodies than slots, a dancer still for 5 s hands her id to another body. Four well-separated dancers with the cap at 4 behave like the validated duo. | high | [regime](audit-2026-11/regime-n-greater-than-2.md) |
| Dusk fade | Only the plate follows the light. It tracks a 20–60 min fade. A change faster than a few minutes makes it stale; ghost veto, foreground hold and belt backing then switch off together, unlogged. The alert's remedy needs an empty wall. | high | [regime](audit-2026-11/regime-dusk-light-drift.md) |
| Roof and floor entries | Edge logic is left/right only. A roof descent gets its point 1.7–2.4 s after appearing (1.1–1.25 s from the side), about 0.25 body-heights off. Pausing with less than half the body inside the ROI means no point. | medium | [regime](audit-2026-11/regime-vertical-entries.md) |

The rules behind these losses are spread over three layers: the detection filters (shadow suppression,
motion cross-validation), the tracker (`_merge_duplicate_tracks` has no co-fed veto, left/right-only edges,
warm-up from zero) and the slot layer (merge of converged slots, static yield, rebind of a slot whose track
missed one frame).

## 4. The estimation core

Per frame, between YOLO and the OSC point: height guard → dedupe → motion cross-validation → exclusion → cold
motion blobs → `DancerTracker` (3,798 lines) → identity slots (1,494 lines, 83 parameters) → OSC.

- **The slot layer is the right kind of estimator** for the requirement: a fixed number of targets, time in
  seconds, typed evidence (track, foreground, belt).
- **The tracker is not.** It is an identity tracker for an unbounded number of people, counted in frames
  (`dt = 1.0`, `core/tracker.py:330`). Its association gates are overridden by rescue rules. About a third of
  its lines serve identity purity and duplicate clean-up; 552 lines of swap correctors are off by default and
  no project enables them.
- **The losses sit at the boundary** between the two, and the slot layer cannot repair it from above.
- **Replacing the tracker is not proven.** The foreground-only prototype of October never releases a point, so
  its coverage is by construction. On the only footage with a real empty-wall plate (the 30 m solo takes), a
  body-sized foreground blob sits far from the walker on 58–90 % of frames.
- **The fair comparison was never run:** the shipped recipe with the plate has not been scored on any
  two-dancer footage.

The judge's ruling ([estimator-panel.md](audit-2026-11/estimator-panel.md)): measure first, delete the dead
code, build a known-N estimator offline only if the baseline misses at the tracker boundary. The judge did not
have §3. With it, and with the owner open to a deep rethink, this audit recommends starting the offline
estimator alongside the baseline work and deciding the switch on labelled footage.

Three structural points apply whichever way that goes:

- **Time.** A frame carries no capture time from camera to pipeline to recorder. The tracker cannot see a
  stall, and a stall inside a recorded take cannot be replayed.
- **Config.** Six hand-written key lists (save, live apply, replay apply, schema, defaults, remote commands)
  disagree: load applies five keys that save drops; replay reads seven the app never applies. Counts: 75 saved
  keys, 303 import-time constants, 83 slot parameters. Nothing in the app compares a project's recipe with the
  validated one, and D33 fills only absent keys.
- **Runtime.** OSC is emitted on the thread that renders the GUI and runs every command. Nothing supervises the
  process. A restart is not a resume: the empty-wall plate is looked up under the previous project's folder,
  and live changes exist only in memory.

## 5. Validation

- All 19 scenario manifests pin intermittent confirm off; the shipped default is on.
- The six emitted-stream fixtures predate the D27 recipe. Switching off smart hold, foreground veto, foreground
  hold or belt backing changes none of them.
- The footage golden compares tracker-internal counters, not what OSC carries.
- CI stops at a collection error on `release` (simulated locally; GitHub not checked), and
  `release-check --tests` returns 0 on a failed suite.
- Four harness tools fail when run, among them the one behind the operator's Auto-tune button.
- With the shipped recipe, the April duo proxy scores 0.96–0.97 on two-point presence and misses on the longest
  hole (1.3–1.5 s) and on coasting (12–14 %).

Detail: [dim-validation.md](audit-2026-11/dim-validation.md).

## 6. Performance

- October's pass worked: on the laptop the CPU motion feed is off the critical path, and YOLO is on it.
- At x@1280 the projection is 29–37 ms of a 50 ms period before TouchDesigner. It is a projection: every laptop
  timing in the repo was taken at imgsz 800.
- CPU after YOLO grows with the number of dancers: 6.7 / 14 / 27 / 40 ms at N = 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 on mgr-ran (the
  laptop's CPU is about 2.5 times faster).
- USB stalls outweigh every millisecond item: 2–8 % of show time has no fresh frame.
- A rectangular engine gains nothing on the 25 m wall ROI (1322×1275); it matters for strip or portrait ROIs.

Detail: [dim-performance.md](audit-2026-11/dim-performance.md).

## 7. Operator interaction

- 97 controls are reachable on the default surface (21–39 on screen at once); expert mode hides 10. The Live
  panel grew from 12 to 21 controls during October.
- Labels, alerts and readiness messages are English with pipeline units; the operator is French-speaking.
- Every alert but one is an 8-second toast. No place states whether the show is healthy, and why not.
- Nothing protects a running show: the app boots in RUN; one click on a recording slot replaces the camera
  with a looping take while RUN and OSC stay green; single-letter shortcuts fire while typing.
- The documented remedy for a light change (replay the empty take and Calibrate, "le spectacle continue")
  stops the camera, resets the tracker and sends `/walldance/clear`.

Detail: [dim-operator-ux.md](audit-2026-11/dim-operator-ux.md).

## 8. Robustness in the field

- Calibrate is not transactional: with someone in frame it pins the weakest enhancement and overwrites the
  plate before Save.
- The 2.5 s camera reopen pre-empts the 5 s stall hold (recorded in PLAN_25M §C.16). Typical 1.7 s stalls are
  covered once D34 is released; a reconnect silently ends a recording.
- A hang is neither recovered nor diagnosed: the watchdog's stack dump fails under the log tee.
- The launcher fetches from the network on every start, and a bad release cannot be rolled back on site.

Detail: [dim-field-robustness.md](audit-2026-11/dim-field-robustness.md),
[dim-runtime-architecture.md](audit-2026-11/dim-runtime-architecture.md).
