## The demo KPI (proposed definition of done, to confirm)

Measured with `replay.py --quality` (slots ON) on a show-like take at 25 m with two dancers, and by eye on TD:

| metric | target | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| two emitted points while two dancers are on the wall | ≥ 95 % of those frames | `emitted` stream vs the per-range N of the take |
| longest hole per dancer | ≤ 1 s | continuity C3 on the emitted stream |
| coasting share | ≤ 10 % of emitted points | `coasting_share` |
| point on the body (within 0.75 h) | ≥ 95 % | `on_dancer` |
| jitter at rest | ≤ 1.5 % of dancer height | `jitter_rest_pct` |
| lag on fast moves | ≤ 80 ms | `lag_ms` |
| frame rate with TD running | 20 fps sustained, GPU < 83 °C | `[Budget]` + `nvidia-smi` on the laptop |

**No baseline exists yet.** The 2026-10-05 Bordeaux takes are a mixed bag (Thomas, 2026-10-06): some are tests
with the IR light off-axis (no belt return), some are blurry, some have the operator walking near the camera (not
the use case), and the number of dancers on the wall is not known per take — so nothing read from them (two-point
presence, holes, belt rates, the IR budget) is a KPI number until each take is classified. Step A1 does that
classification first; the KPI baseline then comes from tonight's protocol takes (`mur25m-ceinture-0610`, slots
4/5/6/9), which were shot for it.
