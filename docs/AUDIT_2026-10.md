## 1. Executive summary

1. **🔴 Prod safety first — the field updater can destroy work on the laptop** (ARCH stream, reproduced in a sandbox).
   - The only committed launcher exe (`launcher/release/WallDanceLauncher.exe`, 2026-03-26) predates the 06-11 safety fix `188d2f7`.
   - It offers "update?" whenever the laptop's HEAD ≠ `origin/main`, **including when the laptop simply has unpushed commits**.
   - Answering **Yes** is a hand-written hard reset: uncommitted tracked edits are lost for good, and local commits are orphaned without a reflog entry.
   - It only watches `main`.
   - Since all June–July development happened on that laptop, its state must be **inventoried and backed up before `main` moves** (§3).
2. **🔴 A reinstall on prod would silently cost TensorRT.**
   - Installers delete `uv.lock`; every dependency is a floor; `uv pip install --upgrade torch` overrides `numpy<2`.
   - Today's resolve gives TensorRT 11.3, which **cannot load the June engines** (serialization 243 ≠ 244).
   - It **cannot build new ones the app's way**: FP16 export now needs `nvidia-modelopt`.
   - The app then falls back to PyTorch (3–7× slower). Readiness reports FAIL but does not block RUN.
   - Any push touching `install.bat` / `pyproject.toml` triggers that reinstall at next launch.
3. **🔴 Show-loss classes on the live path.**
   - Closing the launcher window kills the app within ~5 s: stdout is a launcher pipe.
   - Any uncaught CUDA / tracker / OSC error ends the process. There is no `finally`, no log file (404 `print`s, no `logging`) and no auto-restart.
   - Nothing guards sleep or Windows Update.
4. **Continuity: the passing 300-frame goldens hide the problem, and the drops are made by the tracker/report layer, not by YOLO.**
   - On full recordings with the pinned golden configs:

     | Recording | Coverage | Gaps | OSC ids for one dancer | Other |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | Aerial (slot 4) | **94.2 %** | 22 per minute | **25** | 7 of 16 consecutive 300-frame windows pass |
     | Floor / rope (slot 3) | **57.5 %** | holes up to **30 s** | — | — |
   - On slot 4 an internal track existed on **100 %** of drop frames.
   - Drop frames come from:
     - the frozen-ghost report gate (59 %);
     - track splits (26 %);
     - warm-up decay (11 %).
5. **The operator's "+++ issues on merging tracks (yolo AND motion)" is a concrete mechanism.**
   - A MOG2 blob more than 0.3 h from the YOLO box centre becomes a second detection of the same dancer and can win the assignment.
   - The YOLO skeleton is then discarded (326 frames on slot 4). The track goes skeleton-stale and the frozen gate hides it, while YOLO boxes her at 0.61–0.87.
   - Fixed wall spots also seed tracks that hijack the dancer from up to 520 px away.
6. **Continuity is very recoverable.**
   - Knob changes alone (τ 0.35 + 3 exclusion cells + frozen gate after 15 frames) take slot 4 to **99.5 %** coverage, longest gap 0.51 s.
   - Decoupling "keep emitting" from "first confirmation" takes slot 3 from 57.5 to **93.8 %**.
   - The biggest structural lever is an **output-only known-N "identity slot" layer**: ids go 25 → **1** and coverage to 98.7–99.7 % (simulated offline).
   - It must ship with ghost control. On a textured wall, ~half of the emitted frames sat on wall stains while the count-based scorer still called it 79 % "covered".
7. **IR markers at ankles + wrists confirm presence but don't pin position; add one harness marker.**
   - Recordings are faithful: the live path is Mono8 (not Mono10/12 as the docs said) and slot files are lossless FFV1 copies of it.
   - In a synthetic study on ~7k real skeleton frames, 4 extremity markers cut fast-aerial centroid error during a 10-frame YOLO gap from 0.28 H (hold) to **0.07 H**. On slow climbs and floor work, holding the last position is already as good.
   - What markers give everywhere is **existence/keep-alive**: ghost rejection, static acquisition, no drops. That means a *moderate* Kalman R, not the "tiny R" the June plan assumed.
   - **One harness/hip marker is worth as much as all four extremities.**
   - The biggest physical risks are exposure (~51.6 ms in the corpus ⇒ streaks) and illuminator offset from the lens (§4).
8. **Performance: on the show laptop the CPU motion feed (19.3 ms), not YOLO (11.6 ms), is the critical path**, and fps is source-bound (~19.7).
   - `import ultralytics` silently makes OpenCV single-threaded. Restoring threads is **bit-identical** and saves ~6–7 ms per frame.
   - Ultralytics adds ~4–5 ms of avoidable syncs plus an unused 4.9 MB GPU→CPU copy per frame. That copy may relate to the IDS USB3 stalls (untested hypothesis).
   - Glass→OSC latency is ≈ 79 ms p50 (+~50 ms centroid EMA lag); ~55 ms is reachable.
   - Model/imgsz choice (x vs l, 960 vs 1280) costs ~0 wall time today, so choose on accuracy.
9. **Architecture is sound at the seams.** No import cycles; 82 of 84 GUI actions go through the command queue.
   - The weight is in `tracker.py` (3.6k lines, single class, 44 % covered), `gui.py` (2.9k), `app.py` (2.2k, config applier CC 65).
   - Coverage is 30 %; CI cannot pass (missing `scipy`/`filterpy`); goldens compare aggregate counts only.
   - Replay and the live app apply config in a **different order**, so known-N's `tracker_max_age` search is blind on motion_first projects (BUG-3).
   - The marker and continuity work both fit through two seams: a typed measurement record and an extracted output stage. Neither requires splitting the tracker.
10. **Docs describe 2026-06-25 and the operator has no manual.**
    - README shows the pre-rail UI and a wrong file map. ROADMAP and the engineering record list fixed bugs as open.
    - The GUI tells the operator Aim "captures the clean plate" (it doesn't). `/walldance/clear` is never sent live.
    - Nothing reflects the field test, continuity or ankle/wrist markers.
    - Plan: 6 live docs, including a French `MODE_EMPLOI.md` (TODO-me "+++ Mode d'emploi"), plus `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `TESTING.md` and a repo `CLAUDE.md`.

## 2. Cross-stream resolutions

Where two streams touched the same thing, this is the reconciled position.

| Topic | Streams | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Marker detector placement** | MRK (CPU, inside the motion worker) vs PERF (the motion worker *is* the prod critical path) | Write the detector as a pure function on the mono8 ROI gray, so placement can change. **Land PERF-1 (cv2 threads, bit-identical) first**; then the motion worker is fine at ~1 ms sparse on prod. Fallbacks: the IDS acquisition thread, or GPU on the raw mono tensor (≈0.8–1 ms). Gate: p95 ≤ 2 ms on prod, no change to `process_wall` beyond ±1 ms. |
| **One measurement seam** | CONT-3 (YOLO vs synthetic tiers), MRK interface, ARCH-11 (typed `Measurement`) | **One item, `SEAM-1`:** a typed measurement (`source ∈ {yolo, motion_blob, bridge, marker}`, `r_mult`, credits, `resets_skeleton_age`) plus a per-track **evidence ledger** (`frames_since[src]`, `last_strong_pos`, `ever_emitted`). It replaces the "all-zero confidence = motion" convention. Land it as a pure refactor (goldens byte-identical), then the behaviour changes. |
| **Output / identity stage** | CONT-6 (identity slots) and ARCH-12 (extract the output stage) | **One item:** extract `core/output_stage.py` (finalize → identity slots → smoothing → OSC), then build the slot layer there, output-only, without touching the 3.6k-line tracker. |
| **Coasting policy** | CONT §3.6 and MRK §4 (both measured: velocity coasting is worse than holding) | Move association to **hold-style coasting**, but **only after** the frozen gate stops keying on instantaneous Kalman speed (CONT-4). Doing it first costs 1.1 points of coverage. |
| **OSC additions** | CONT `/dancer/state`, MRK `/dancer/source` + `/dancer/markers` | **One** additive, opt-in message `/walldance/dancer/state [id, src, state, age_s]`, with `src ∈ {skeleton, marker, motion, coast}` and `state ∈ {live, coasting, lost}`. `/markers` is a separate opt-in. Both need TouchDesigner/operator confirmation and a new OSC_CONTRACT §D. Default contract unchanged. |
| **Test oracle** | CONT-7 (continuity metrics, long spans), ARCH-13 (identity goldens), ARCH-5 / PERF-0 (engine-locked goldens) | **One item, `TEST-1`:** the C1–C10 continuity metrics in `scoring.py`, full-length scenarios, per-frame emitted-id goldens, and an engine/TRT/ultralytics version stamp on every golden. This is the prerequisite for any tracker change. |
| **Config applier** | CONT BUG-3, ARCH-10 | Quick fix now (replay applies `tracker_max_age` *after* `tracking_mode`, matching the app). Single shared applier later (ARCH-10) with a parity test. |
| **Dependencies / TRT** | ARCH-4/5, PERF-0 | **The baseline is prod's current stack** (capture `uv pip freeze` first). Commit a lock; installers do `uv sync --frozen`. Prefer pinning prod's TensorRT (probably 10.x) over moving to 11 + modelopt; decide once the freeze is known. Engines get a version stamp, and a mismatch becomes a loud "rebuild" prompt rather than a silent PyTorch run. |
| **`TrackingMode`** | ROADMAP §4 says "remove the vestigial enum"; ARCH found it live in 4/12 projects; REQ asks for a motion fallback | **Keep it.** Drop "remove TrackingMode" from the simplification backlog. Build the small-figure motion lane separately (CONT-8), gated on a fixed background and the N budget. |
| **Exposure** | MRK (≤ 25 ms or markers streak), PERF (shorter exposure = −12 ms latency), CONT (YOLO already starves on dark scenes) | Shorter exposure only **with more IR (HW P1.3)**. Otherwise YOLO confidence drops and continuity gets worse. Gate any exposure change on the continuity suite. Marker footage: shoot at ≤ 25 ms (Aim) with AE/AG off, and record one exposure ladder (§4). |
| **Dev-box goldens** | PERF (TRT 11 rebuild shifts the aerial golden slightly: `gate_rejections` 79→77, still PASS A) | Goldens are per-engine. dev37 numbers are a **dev reference only**; the prod laptop's engine is the source of truth. |
